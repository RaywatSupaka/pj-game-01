# Discovery 0003 — Roster synergy auto-battler

Status: collecting design answers; no gameplay implementation authorized by this note
Source: user's change from attack/defense to race-based roster building

## Confirmed direction

Remove alternating attack/defense. Roll for characters, combine races/traits, position units and place objects.

## First questions

1. Keep the previous independent run / prepare-without-timer / Ready direction, or move to synchronous timed lobbies?
2. Should each character have race plus class (e.g. dwarf + engineer), or race only?
3. Should objects grant positional buffs, attack, block paths, or combine several roles?

## Proposals to discuss

Race plus class; positional support objects first; count unique characters for trait thresholds; prototype with three races and six to nine units.
These are assistant suggestions, not accepted decisions.

## Follow-up before plan

Define duplicates/upgrades, shop pool and roll odds, economy, team/bench/object capacity, victory/draw rules, board/movement, synergy thresholds/stacking, save/resume and observable playtest criteria.
Keep core scope small even though the long-term roster may be large.
